RISK OPERATIONS

Risk alerts

Understand which signals are triggered, and why. Alerts are illustrative and rule-based.

Active signal feed

Updated when scenario parameters or data change

Rule definitions

Transparent, configurable concept rules

R01
Reserve backing

Critical when current or stressed reserve coverage is below 100%.

R02
Liquidity gap

Warning when redemption demand exceeds accessible funds within the selected window.

R03
Low reserve buffer

Watch when coverage is 100–102% (illustrative threshold).

R04
Disclosure latency

Information warning when the most recent snapshot is more than seven calendar days old.

These thresholds are project assumptions. They are not HKMA supervisory alert categories.
PROJECT EXPERIMENT

Monitoring frequency comparison

Compare detection delay under different hypothetical observation schedules.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXPERIMENT
This is a controlled observation-schedule experiment. It does not measure HKMA reporting performance or prove off-chain reserve visibility.

How quickly would a risk be observed?

Simulation of the next scheduled observation after a user-specified shock

Assumptions

Transparent experiment design

01
Hourly observation

Checks occur at the top of each hour.

02
Daily observation

Checks occur each calendar day at 09:00 HKT.

03
Weekly observation

Checks occur each Monday at 09:00 HKT.

More frequent observation can reduce detection delay, but it cannot fix unverifiable or stale off-chain reserve disclosures.

What this demonstrates

With identical underlying risk events and immediate alerting at each observation, increasing monitoring frequency reduces the maximum time an adverse change can go unnoticed. This supports the monitoring design concept, not a claim that StableProof has live access to bank reserves.
